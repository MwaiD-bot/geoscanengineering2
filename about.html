About GeoScan

Built around evidence, not assumptions.

GeoScan Engineering is focused on practical subsurface investigation, combining geophysics, geotechnical methods and laboratory evidence to support better engineering and geological decisions.

Field-ready

Investigation programs are planned around real site constraints, target depths, safety considerations and decision requirements.

Laboratory-led

Laboratory evidence adds measurable information about soil and rock behaviour to the field dataset.

Decision-focused

Our reporting emphasizes what the data means for foundations, roads, boreholes, resources and subsurface risk.

Our commitment.

Clear communication, traceable observations and technically grounded interpretation are central to the way we approach investigations.
