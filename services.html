What we do

Technical services built around subsurface evidence.

Our investigations combine field observations, geophysical measurements and laboratory characterization to produce practical, defensible information for engineering and geoscience decisions.

Borehole Geophysical Surveys

Downhole geophysical logging provides continuous information that complements drilling records and cuttings.

  • Electrical resistivity / conductivity
  • Natural gamma and spontaneous potential
  • Lithological and structural interpretation
  • Integration with drilling and geological records

Geotechnical Surveys

Ground investigations for buildings, roads and civil infrastructure, from reconnaissance through reporting.

  • Site reconnaissance and investigation planning
  • Subsurface profiling and sampling
  • Foundation and pavement investigations
  • Geotechnical interpretation and reporting

Soil Analysis

Laboratory testing and classification of soils to support engineering assessment and design decisions.

  • Particle size distribution
  • Atterberg limits and soil classification
  • Moisture content and density
  • Engineering interpretation and reporting
