Our approach

One workflow.
Multiple evidence streams.

Good subsurface decisions come from connecting observations rather than relying on a single dataset. GeoScan brings field, geophysical and laboratory evidence into one interpretation.

01 / PLAN

Define the question

Scope the engineering or geological objective, depth and decision requirements.

02 / ACQUIRE

Measure the ground

Collect borehole, geotechnical, geophysical and representative laboratory data.

03 / ANALYZE

Characterize materials

Compare field observations, measurements and laboratory results to identify patterns and uncertainty.

04 / INTERPRET

Deliver decisions

Translate subsurface evidence into clear engineering and geoscience recommendations.

Why integrate
the evidence?

Different investigation methods answer different questions. Their combination gives a more complete picture of the ground and reduces avoidable uncertainty.

Field context

Site observations establish the geological, structural and engineering context in which measurements are interpreted.

Geophysical continuity

Geophysical measurements help reveal changes between discrete observations and can guide follow-up investigation.

Laboratory confidence

Laboratory tests provide measurable material properties that strengthen classification and engineering interpretation.
